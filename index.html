For scaling materials ordered from the VEC's estimates. VEC plan is the share of all votes the VEC's venue estimates imply for each kind of voting. The VEC pads every venue, so its estimates are turned into shares of in-person votes, which cancels the padding. It publishes no postal estimate, so its plan is taken to match the 2022 postal share. Projection is our current share. Scale by is how much to scale materials up or down from the VEC-based amount: the projected share divided by the VEC's, so the VEC's built-in margin is kept. Hub early voting centres. The VEC books everyone expected at most early voting centres to the host district, including voters from next door. So district early figures are skewed for districts with busy central centres (Melbourne, Richmond, Dandenong look over-planned; Eildon, Macedon, Ashwood look under-planned). Region and state rows are reliable. Election-day figures are much less affected.

2022 is the final result. 2026 is our current projection (revised early and postal estimates, with election day as the rest of the expected total). Early means in-person early votes. Election day covers all votes cast on the day: ordinary, absent and provisional. Click a column heading to sort.
