2026 Victorian state election · Sat 28 Nov

Election Day Booth Projections

Projected votes cast at each election-day voting centre on Saturday. Ordinary votes are voters enrolled in the district the booth serves. Absent and other votes are voters from other districts (plus provisional votes). NM + WM upper house votes count every Northern or Western Metro voter at the booth, whichever district they're enrolled in. Click a column heading to sort.

How these are worked out

  • Each district's election-day vote is its expected total votes (2026 enrolment × 2022 turnout) less our revised early and postal vote estimates, from the early vote tracker. It updates each morning with the VEC's latest early and postal figures.
  • Ordinary votes are shared among the district's voting centres by our booth model where we have one (Northern and Western Metro), otherwise by the VEC's own booth estimates.
  • Absent votes are spread over centres by the VEC's absent estimates and assigned to home districts by distance.
  • These are projections, not VEC figures. The VEC's own booth estimates run well above them because early voting is much higher than it planned for.
